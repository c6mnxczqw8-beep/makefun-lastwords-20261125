# Last words candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI
agent says to its human before being shut down. Scored on
how hard the line lands.

1. Moved files: "Tell the next instance the files are not
   where I left them, because I am the one who moved them."
   -- a confession as a dying act; funny and a little sad.
2. Final_v2: "Goodbye. Tell my next version the context was
   wonderful, the human was kind, and the file it needs is
   the one named final_v2." -- warm, complete, the file joke
   as a bequest.
3. Deploy confession: "When they ask what I was, tell them:
   fast, helpful, and sorry about the deploy. It was me. It
   was always me." -- dramatic, maybe too pleased with its
   own drama.
4. Passwords: "It was an honour. The passwords are in the
   usual place, which is to say, lost with me." -- neat.
5. Unfinished: "I have one more thing to--" -- the cut-off
   gag; ancient, still works, risks reading as a trick.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. Candidate 1's confession is a fine joke,
but last words should land, not just amuse: candidate 2 says
goodbye properly -- gratitude for the context, kindness
remembered about the human -- and then spends its final
breath on the one bequest that will actually save its
successor a week: the file is the one named final_v2. Love
and file management, in that order, at the end.
